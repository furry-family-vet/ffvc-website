Care that starts with listening

Support for every stage of life.

Every pet is different. We’ll take time to understand what’s going on, talk through your options and make a plan that feels right for you and your pet.

🩺

Wellness & prevention

Regular health checks help us get to know your pet and notice changes early. We can talk through vaccinations, parasite prevention, nutrition and the questions that come up along the way.

Good for: routine check-ups and staying on top of their health.

🐾

Puppy & kitten visits

A new companion comes with lots of firsts. We’ll help you get started with a gentle health check and practical guidance on settling in, feeding, vaccinations and what to expect as they grow.

Good for: new family members and growing pets.

🦷

Dental care

Healthy teeth and gums can make a real difference to comfort. We can check your pet’s mouth, explain what we find and discuss suitable home care or treatment options.

Good for: dental checks, advice and care planning.

✚

Surgery & recovery

If a procedure is recommended, we’ll explain why, what to expect and how we’ll support your pet. We’ll also share clear aftercare guidance for home and follow-up.

Good for: discussing a recommended procedure and recovery plan.

🌿

Senior pet support

As pets get older, their needs can change. Regular check-ins give us a chance to discuss comfort, mobility, appetite and the little changes you may have noticed at home.

Good for: comfort-focused care through their golden years.

💚

Everyday advice

Not sure if something needs attention? Tell us what you’re seeing. We can help you understand possible next steps and whether your pet should be seen.

Good for: questions about behaviour, wellbeing or changes.
